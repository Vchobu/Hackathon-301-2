# Hackathon Bank

## About the bank

Hackathon Bank is a fictional bank used for this demo credit assistant.
The assistant can provide general information from this document, but it cannot
open an account, submit documents, or make a final lending decision.

## Personal loans

Hackathon Bank offers unsecured personal loans for personal expenses. The demo
assistant supports a 36-month term only. Loan amounts, approval, and the final
offer depend on the application and must be confirmed by a bank advisor.
This document does not specify a maximum loan amount.

## Application requirements

Applicants should be at least 18 years old and provide information about their
income, requested amount, housing situation, existing debt payments, and credit
history. Identity and banking documents may be requested by a bank advisor
during the formal application process.

## Fees and repayment

The interest rate, total repayment amount, origination fees, and late-payment
terms are not fixed in this demo document. They must be confirmed in the formal
offer before signing. Early repayment conditions should also be checked with a
bank advisor.

## Support

For questions about documents, formal applications, rates, fees, or account
services, contact a Hackathon Bank advisor. The chatbot can only
provide the general information contained in this document and perform the
separate preliminary model assessment.
